# Write and test subject lines

Purpose: Get more of the right people to open without fake urgency.

Steps:
1. State the email's one goal.
2. Write 10 subject lines across curiosity, benefit, story and direct.
3. Cut any that the email does not deliver on.
4. Pick 2 to test.
5. Record the result in the test log.

Output: Subject line list plus test pick

Checks before you finish:
- No misleading lines
- Test result logged
