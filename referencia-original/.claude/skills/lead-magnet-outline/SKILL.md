# Outline a lead magnet

Purpose: Design a free resource that solves one urgent problem fast.

Steps:
1. Name the one problem and the reader.
2. Write the promise in the title.
3. Outline sections that deliver the promise.
4. Choose the format that is fastest to use.
5. Link the last section to your paid offer naturally.

Output: Lead magnet outline

Checks before you finish:
- Title promise is delivered in full
- Can be used in under 30 minutes
