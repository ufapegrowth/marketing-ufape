# Build a content calendar

Purpose: Turn pillars and goals into a realistic 4-week schedule.

Steps:
1. List pillars and the goal of each.
2. Pick formats and channels.
3. Fill 4 weeks with topics, formats, owners and dates.
4. Mark which pieces get repurposed.
5. Leave one open slot a week for timely topics.

Output: 4-week calendar table

Checks before you finish:
- Volume matches capacity
- Every piece maps to a pillar
