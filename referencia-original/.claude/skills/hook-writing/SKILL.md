# Write hooks that are true

Purpose: Write first lines that stop the scroll and are paid off by the body.

Steps:
1. Find the tension, number or surprise in the content.
2. Write 10 hooks across 6 types.
3. Cut any hook the body does not pay off.
4. Score clarity, curiosity and truth from 1 to 5.
5. Keep the top 3.

Output: Hook list with scores

Checks before you finish:
- Every hook is true to the body
- Top 3 are under 15 words
