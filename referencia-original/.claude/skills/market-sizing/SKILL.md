# Size a market without made-up numbers

Purpose: Turn public evidence into a defensible view of who buys and how many.

Steps:
1. List buyer groups and how you could count each one.
2. Find countable sources: directories, registries, platform counts, reports.
3. Estimate a low and high count per group and show the maths.
4. Note the share you could realistically reach in 12 months and why.
5. Write the confidence level and what would change it.

Output: Sizing table with low, high, source and confidence per group

Checks before you finish:
- Every number has a source or is labelled an assumption
- Low and high are both shown
