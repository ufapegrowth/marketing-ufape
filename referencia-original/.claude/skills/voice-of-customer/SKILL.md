# Build a voice of customer bank

Purpose: Capture the exact words buyers use so every asset sounds like them.

Steps:
1. Collect raw text: reviews, calls, surveys, support tickets, forum posts.
2. Highlight phrases about pains, desires, objections and outcomes.
3. Group phrases by theme and keep them verbatim.
4. Mark the 10 most repeated phrases.
5. Save the bank in the knowledge folder for every writing agent.

Output: Voice of customer bank grouped by theme with top 10 phrases

Checks before you finish:
- Quotes are verbatim and anonymised
- Each theme has at least 3 quotes
